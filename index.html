Before the First Door

I ask questions I do not ask,
and hide my answer in plain sight.
You will meet me again, and again.
Name me.

Three gates guard the way. This is the first.

The second gate

A lamp is kept at the foot of the path for those who are truly lost. Reach for it only as a last resort.

You have named me. Now light my way.
Some lanterns burn along the path, and some still sleep.
Wake one, and its neighbours stir with it.
Light them all, and the road will speak.

The third gate

The last gate is a river of light.
It enters from the left and means to leave by the right.
Turn the stones until the light runs through.

You have found the way in.

Five doors will come to you, one at a time, roughly a week apart. Each arrives as a link, from me, and from no one else.

Behind each door waits a puzzle of a different kind. Solve it, and it hands you a single digit: a key.

Write every key down, in the order you find it. Memory is a poor vault.

Nothing beyond the page is needed, only patience and good light. Every answer can be found from what you are given.

Beneath each solved door you will find a word for the road. Read it twice. It is about what to bring.

When the fifth door has closed behind you, a sixth will open just before you leave. It will tell you what to do with your keys, and ask for nothing else.

What waits behind it is worth the climb.

The first door opens soon.
