The Second Key

Six strangers sit at one long table.
Each offers a word in a tongue that is not yours.
Give it back as you would say it.
Only some letters stand apart, and they keep an order of their own.

The table has spoken a name.
Give me its number.

Before the table can speak, the frost-lace must be filled.
Every clue hides its answer in plain sight: a double meaning, a joining, a secret tucked inside other words.
Fill it, and a lock will open.
