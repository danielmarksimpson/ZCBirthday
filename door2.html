The Second Key

Ten windows across, ten windows down.
The numbers at the edges tell how many burn in an unbroken run,
and between each run, at least one stays dark.
Light only what must be lit, and a shape will remember itself.
When the picture is true, a door will open.

Tap to light a window. Tap again to mark it dark. Once more to clear it.

The shadows hold.
What do they spell?

Key two is yours: 2

The air is getting thinner. Three more to find.

Keep both numbers close, in the order you found them.

A word for the road

Amid all that cold, there is warmth to be found, and water that asks very little of you but a little less clothing.
Bring a piece of summer with you in miniature, and better still, in two pieces.
