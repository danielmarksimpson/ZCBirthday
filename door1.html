The First Key

Before you climb, you must step back.
Not far: a general’s count of three.
What was said is hidden in plain sight;
what is hidden is how you will feel.

ZKHUH WKHDL UJURZ VWKLQ WKHZD WHUVW DBVZD UPDQG WKHZR UOGJU RZVVL OHQW

The final word carries more than meaning; its count unlocks the way.

Key one is yours: 6

Four more are on their way, and each will arrive wearing a different disguise.

Keep this number close. You will need all of them in the end.

A word for the road

Across the sea, winter keeps its own court, and you will be a guest in it.
Dress as one who means to be welcome: a hat for the crown, a coat for the heart, and gloves for the hands that will want to hold on.
For the ground beneath you, boots that remember how to walk. You will be glad of them.
