The Fourth Key

The night is thick, and your lantern lights only a few steps in any direction.
Somewhere ahead, a green glow marks the way out, though it will not show itself from afar.
What you pass, you will only half remember.

Five rites. Five hours. Five numbers.
Each rite is taken once, at its own hour,
and answers to a number that no other shares.

10  22  34  55  80

  1. Haze keeps a lower number than Moss.
  2. The highest number greets the day.
  3. Slate answers to the lowest number.
  4. The lowest follows the highest without delay.
  5. Moss is neither the highest nor the lowest.
  6. Only Slate is lower than Reed.
  7. Nothing follows Reed.
  8. Haze follows Moss without delay.
HourNumber

Every table has a ceiling, and a number clings to it.
At what hour does its rite arrive?

Key four is yours: 1

Two locks remain, and then the way opens.

Keep all four numbers close, in the order you found them.

A word for the road

Leave a little of yourself empty, for something is waiting to fill it.
Come hungry. The table where you are going has been laid with care, and it does not intend to be ignored.

The sea remembers what the stone refuses to say.
