The Third Key

A picture has been shaken from its frame and lies in nine pieces.
One piece is missing, and the space it left lets the others move.
Slide them back, and see what the window was looking at.

Ten windows across, ten windows down.
The numbers at the edges tell how many burn in an unbroken run,
and between each run, at least one stays dark.
Light only what must be lit, and a shape will remember itself.
When the picture is true, a door will open.

Tap to light a window. Tap again to mark it dark. Once more to clear it.

The shadows hold.
What do they spell?

Key three is yours: 2

The air is getting thinner. Two more to find.

Keep all three numbers close, in the order you found them.

A word for the road

Amid all that cold, there is warmth to be found, and water that asks very little of you but a little less clothing.
Bring a piece of summer with you in miniature, or better still, two.
And above all, come prepared to relax: leave the hurry at the door, and bring only your willingness to be still.
And wander freely: let unfamiliar places lead you, and let the strange and the new teach you sensations you have not yet met.

Seek the poet in marble, who keeps his vigil over a market of lanterns long after his last verse. Then rise above him, to where the gold-maned are said to be born, and the water forgets what season it is. Across the valley, pillars of earth wear hats of stone, as patient as chess pieces, and they have stood there longer than anyone has been counting.
