The Fifth Key

Four rings of stone rest upon the first pillar, the greatest below.
Carry them all to the last, one ring at a time.
No greater ring may ever rest upon a lesser one.

Two riddles stand in your way.
Name them both, and the lock will turn.

I am the heaviest thing a room can hold and the lightest to give.
I break the moment you name me.
The snow brings me in handfuls, and a good friend can share me without a word.
What am I?

Named

I climb without legs and vanish without a trace.
Fire is my mother and water is my father.
The world tightens you; I am what loosens.
What am I?

Named

Lay one over the other.
How many letters stand on common ground?

Key five is yours: 2

Five are in your hand now, and one door remains.

Hold them in the order you found them. One more door waits.

A word for the road

Pack one thing to be looked at in.
The evenings will deserve it.

The place has two names, and neither is wrong.
You will stand before a man older than the pyramids, and he will not mind the queue.
